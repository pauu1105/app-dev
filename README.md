# My Favorite Movie & Series

Here is a quick overview of my all-time favorite animated movie and anime series.

---

## Favorite Movie: *Coraline*

> "Be careful what you wish for."

**Coraline** is a 2009 stop-motion dark fantasy film directed by Henry Selick. It follows a young girl who discovers an idealized parallel world behind a secret door in her new home, only to realize it hides a sinister secret.

### Why I Love It:
* The visuals of this movie is amazing because the movements and emotions of each character are clean and expressive.
* The atmosphere blends whimsy and eerie suspense effortlessly.
* The story itself is good because it taught me that *perfect is'nt always good*.

---

## Favorite Series: *One-Punch Man*

**One-Punch Man** is an action-comedy anime series about **Saitama**, a superhero who has trained so hard that he can defeat any opponent with a single punch—leaving him bored from a lack of a real challenge.

### Key Highlights:
1. **Top-Tier Animation:** Incredible, high-energy fight scenes.
2. **Humor & Parody:** A hilarious take on classic superhero tropes.
3. **Memorable Cast:** Features iconic heroes like *Genos*, *King*, and *Mumen Rider*.
